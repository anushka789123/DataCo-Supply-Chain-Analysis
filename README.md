# DataCo-Supply-Chain-Analysis
Power BI supply chain &amp; sales analysis dashboard.

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, customers, supply chain operations, and delivery efficiency.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the DataCo Supply Chain dataset to identify business trends, performance drivers, and operational issues.

The Power BI report provides interactive dashboards for exploring sales, profit, customers, orders, markets, products, and delivery performance.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of business performance through:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Sales trends
- Regional and category performance

### 2. Sales & Customer Analysis
Analyzes:
- Sales and profit by customer segment
- Sales by market
- Sales by country
- Sales by customer

### 3. Operations & Logistics Analysis
Focuses on delivery and operational performance:
- On-Time Delivery
- Late Orders
- Average Delivery Days
- Shipping Mode Performance
- Regional Late Orders
- Department-level Late Orders

### 4. Business Insights & Recommendations
Summarizes key findings from the analysis and translates them into actionable business recommendations.

## Key Business Insights

- **Consumer Segment:** Generates the highest sales at **$19.10M** and profit at **$2.08M**.
- **Leading Markets:** Europe generates **$10.87M** and LATAM generates **$10.28M** in sales.
- **Delivery Performance:** Overall on-time delivery is **42.67%**, with Second Class performing lowest at **20.07%**.
- **Late Orders:** Fan Shop has approximately **23K** late orders, followed by Apparel with approximately **19K**.
- **Top Category:** Fishing generates approximately **$6.93M** in sales.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Delivery** — Reduce delays, particularly for Second Class shipping.
2. **Optimize Fan Shop** — Improve inventory and fulfillment processes.
3. **Strengthen Core Markets** — Expand and retain customers in Europe and LATAM.
